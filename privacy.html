Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Applies to all apps and websites published by OdysseyX LLC

Who we are

OdysseyX LLC ("OdysseyX", "we", "us") is a limited liability company organized in the State of Wyoming, USA. We publish mobile and web applications. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. If you contact us by email, we keep your message and address so we can reply.
  • Information collected by our apps. Our apps are designed to work on your device. Unless an app's own in-app notice says otherwise, we do not collect personal data, create accounts, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Purchase information. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive anonymized transaction records but never your payment card details.
  • Crash and performance data. If you have opted in to share analytics with Apple or Google, we may receive aggregated, non-identifying crash reports to help fix bugs.

How we use information

Only to provide and improve our apps, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Third parties

Our apps are distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which have their own privacy policies. Where a specific app uses an additional third-party service (for example, a cloud sync or AI provider), that app will say so in its listing or inside the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request. You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you by emailing support@odysseyx.app.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted inside the relevant app.
